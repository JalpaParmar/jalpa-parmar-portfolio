02 / CHECK THE EVIDENCE

Five views of the feedback

Counts describe reviews, not users or task failure rates. Compare sample sizes before comparing averages.

The fictional versions are associated with successive months in this dataset. Time and version effects cannot be separated here. A pattern after a release is a question to investigate, not proof of causation.

03 / FRAME THE OPPORTUNITY

Where I would start the conversation

Full dataset · synthetic planning assumptions · investigation order, not a committed roadmap.

How the discussion score works

Score = feedback share × 100 × impact × business relevance × confidence ÷ effort. Feedback share is the count of non-positive reviews in the opportunity’s theme divided by all 180 reviews. Impact and relevance use 1–5; effort uses 1–5 with larger values meaning more work. Confidence is a planning weight: 0.5 (low), 0.75 (medium), 1 (high).

I set confidence to 0.5 because reviews alone are weak evidence for a solution. All other ratings are hypothetical judgments. The score helps structure discussion. It does not replace product judgment or make accessibility needs optional.
