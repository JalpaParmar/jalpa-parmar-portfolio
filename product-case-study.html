SmartServe

Original Product Management Case Study

A greenfield product case study demonstrating product discovery, MVP definition, prioritization, roadmap thinking, user stories and success metrics.

01

Problem

Small service businesses need a simpler way to manage customer requests, appointment changes and routine service communication.

02

Solution

Define a focused MVP, prioritize capabilities using structured criteria, map user journeys and establish outcome-oriented product metrics before implementation.

03

What this demonstrates

Problem framing · PRD thinking · personas · RICE prioritization · roadmap · user stories · KPIs.

PORTFOLIO NOTE
