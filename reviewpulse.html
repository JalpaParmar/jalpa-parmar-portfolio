PORTFOLIO CASE STUDY

ReviewPulse AI

Mobile Customer Review Intelligence

An original analytics concept for collecting public/sample mobile review data and turning ratings, versions, dates and review text into product signals.

01

Problem

Raw app-store feedback is high-volume and difficult for product and engineering teams to turn into prioritized decisions.

02

Solution

A Python-based pipeline that normalizes review data, surfaces rating trends and themes, and produces product-oriented insight summaries using synthetic/public data.

03

What this demonstrates

Customer voice · product analytics · Python · trend analysis · prioritization · mobile product understanding.
