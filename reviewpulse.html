ReviewPulse AI

Mobile Customer Review Intelligence

An original analytics concept for collecting public/sample mobile review data and turning ratings, versions, dates and review text into product signals.

What comes next

This original interface study shows the intended direction. Future walkthroughs will explore the proposed workflow, product decisions and measures of success.

01

Problem

Raw app-store feedback is high-volume and difficult for product and engineering teams to turn into prioritized decisions.

02

Solution

A proposed workflow for organizing sample customer feedback, surfacing rating trends and recurring themes, and informing product priorities.

03

What this demonstrates

Customer voice · product analytics · trend analysis · prioritization · mobile product understanding.

PORTFOLIO NOTE

Independent by design.

This concept preview was created independently using synthetic/sample data and original code. It demonstrates approaches informed by professional experience without reproducing proprietary employer systems or data. The working application is planned for a later phase.
