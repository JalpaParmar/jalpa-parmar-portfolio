Delivery360

Executive Project & Product Health

A synthetic delivery dashboard concept that brings sprint execution, release readiness, quality, risks, dependencies and leadership communication into one view.

01

Problem

Project health is often scattered across multiple tools, making leadership reporting slow and inconsistent.

02

Solution

A unified KPI model with synthetic delivery data, risk indicators and AI-assisted executive summaries designed around decision-making rather than raw metrics.

03

What this demonstrates

Governance · KPI design · release management · risk management · executive communication · analytics.

PORTFOLIO NOTE

Independent by design.

This is an early portfolio concept. Working demos and detailed artifacts are planned. Implementations will use original code and synthetic or public information; no proprietary employer code, data, screenshots or internal documentation.
